# Generalized Linear Mode Connectivity for Transformers

**arXiv:** 2506.22712 (Jun 2025)

## Abstract

Extends Linear Mode Connectivity (Frankle 2020) to transformers under four classes of weight symmetries: permutation, sign, scaling, and general invertible maps. Frames these as a hierarchy of LMC equivalence relations.

## Why this matters for the LoRA-LMC thesis

This paper places **GL(r) reparameterization symmetry** of LoRA into a recognizable taxonomy reviewers will know — it is the "general invertible map" class in their hierarchy. Citing this paper allows the thesis to position LoRA-LMC cleanly within an existing LMC framework rather than appearing to invent the framing from scratch.

## Differentiator

- Generalized LMC for Transformers is **full-network**, not LoRA.
- They handle the four symmetry classes for full-model permutation/sign/scaling — never the LoRA `(B, A)` factorization.
- No trajectory analysis; endpoint LMC only.
- No three-region TRS decomposition.

## How to use in the thesis

In related work: "We instantiate the 'general invertible map' class of [Generalized LMC] specifically for LoRA, where `GL(r)` acts on the factorization rather than on a full-model parameter rearrangement, and pair this with the TRS three-region decomposition to isolate the behavior-bearing subspace."

# CopRA — A Progressive LoRA Training Strategy

**arXiv:** 2410.22911 (Oct 2024)

## Abstract

Progressive training strategy for LoRA adapters: gradually expand active rank or unmask adapter columns over training. Aims to improve generalization and merging of LoRA endpoints.

## Why this matters for the LoRA-LMC thesis

Same shape as CoTo (2506.05713) — a *training-method* paper aimed at making LoRA endpoints play well together. Reviewers familiar with CopRA will pattern-match the thesis to it; the introduction must distinguish *measuring trajectory geometry of vanilla LoRAs* from *training adapters that merge better*.

## Differentiator

CopRA changes the training dynamics; the thesis *observes* the existing dynamics under GL(r)-canonical coordinates. CopRA has no:
- GL(r) gauge fix
- Region 1/2/3 split
- Grassmannian trajectory analysis
- LoRA-LMC framing
- trajectory-shape downstream prediction.

# Spectral Edge Dynamics of Training Trajectories

**arXiv:** 2603.15678 (Mar 2026)

## Abstract

Rolling-window SVD over the parameter-update trajectory of full networks (TinyStories 51M, GPT-2 124M). The leading singular value of the windowed update sequence — the "spectral edge" — exhibits a universal three-phase pattern (rise / plateau / collapse). The collapse provides early-warning prediction of grokking.

## Why this matters for the LoRA-LMC thesis

This is the **closest methodological cousin** to E2 (the trajectory section). Their rolling-window-SVD spectral-edge statistic is essentially the T1 phase-detection instrument the thesis needs — borrowed and ported to LoRAs in the GL(r)-quotient.

## Differentiator

- SED is **full-network**, not LoRA. No GL(r) gauge symmetry to handle.
- They use SVD of *parameter updates* (`Δθ_t = θ_{t+1} − θ_t`), not GL(r)-canonical `(B, A)`.
- No three-region (Region 1/2/3) decomposition; spectral edge alone.
- No notion of intruder dims, W₀-orthogonality, task-shared subspace.
- No downstream-property prediction (mergeability, forgetting).

The thesis can *cite SED as motivation*, *borrow the rolling-window phase statistic for T1*, and ship a LoRA-specific extension with the gauge fix and region split that SED's full-network setting can't accommodate.

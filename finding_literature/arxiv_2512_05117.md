# Universal Weight Subspace Hypothesis (UWSH)

**arXiv:** 2512.05117 (Dec 2025)

## Abstract

Trains 500 Mistral-7B LoRAs, 500 ViTs, and 50 LLaMA-8B fine-tunes; uses HOSVD plus Grassmannian principal-angle distances to argue all fine-tunes concentrate in a single low-dimensional shared subspace — the "universal weight subspace."

## Why this matters for the LoRA-LMC thesis

UWSH looks scary at first glance because it shares vocabulary (Grassmannian, principal angles, fine-tune subspace). On close reading, it is **endpoint-only and identifies one universal subspace**, conflating what the thesis splits into Region 1 (universal fiber, shared) and Region 2 (task-specific signal).

## How to use UWSH in the thesis

Reframe their finding: **UWSH's universal subspace ≈ Region 1 of the TRS three-region decomposition.** Their result then becomes corroborating evidence for the existence of the universal fiber (Region 1) — a known, expected structure that does *not* carry behavior. The thesis's contribution beyond UWSH:

- Region 2 (task-specific, above-MP, W₀-orthogonality split): UWSH does not isolate this.
- Trajectory analysis: UWSH is endpoint-only.
- LoRA-LMC framing: UWSH does not formulate or test mode connectivity.
- Behavior carried by Region 2, not Region 1: the C2 measurement directly contradicts the implicit UWSH reading that "universal subspace = where it all happens."

## Differentiator

Endpoint vs trajectory. One subspace vs three regions. Existence claim vs predictive claim.

## Class

B — adjacent. Useful as a baseline for C2 (regress benchmark vector on UWSH-style universal subspace alone → expect ≈ 0 explained variance, validating Region 2 as the behavioral coordinate).
